Fast NTT: An Adaptation of Fast-Fourier Transform to the Number Theoretic Transform

Trong cac phan truoc, cac trinh bay ve NTT va INTT transformation van co do phuc tap kha lon O(n2)O(n^2)O(n2), vi vay khong co su khac biet giua phuong phap truyen thong cua negacyclic convolution. Tuy nhien vi NTT la mot phan dac biet cua DFT nen optimize tren DFT van co the duoc ap dung vao NTT. Mot ki thuat toi uu kinh dien la Fast-Fourier Transform (FFT) duoc de xuat boi Cooley-Tukey va Gentleman-Sande. Ca hai deu su dung butterflies divide va conquer technique de giam do phuc tap con O(nlog⁡n)O(n\log n)O(nlogn).

De giam do phuc tap duoc nhu the ta su dung "divide va conquer" techniques bang cach su dung tinh chat tuan hoan-periodicity va doi xung-symmetry cua ζ\zetaζ:

  • Tuan hoan: ζk+2⋅n=ζk\zeta^{k+2\cdot n} = \zeta^kζk+2⋅n=ζk.
  • Doi xung: ζk+n=−ζk\zeta^{k+n} = -\zeta^kζk+n=−ζk.

Voi kkk la so nguyen khong am. Phep tinh nnn diem NTT va INTT co the chia lam n/2n/2n/2 diem. Tuy nhien ki thuat chia nay co doi phan kho.

Voi INTT, thay vi chia tong cua no thong qua chi so chan le, no duoc tach bang nua tren nua duoi cua phep cong:

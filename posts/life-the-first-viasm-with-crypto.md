## Motivation and Overview

Hi guys, this is my first time participating a event for Crypto (Despite I have studied it for a while), and it was also my first time traveling to Ha Noi (our capital city). 

In this blog, I want to share everyone my experience and what I learned from this event. And then, I will share some of my first impressions about Ha Noi, the city where Viasm was held.

## What I learned from Viasm 

In this event, I learned a lot of things about crypto, especially in the field of cryptography, a lot of new knowledge, all letures in [link](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1f8ZPlfRh5_QkQDKUXH3WXJcQbpOXikNA), the courses included: 

- Course 1 (12h): Advanced Cryptographic Protocols
    + Confirmed Lecturers: Ilaria Chilotti, Duong Hieu Phan, Ky Nguyen

- Course 2 (12h): Quantum Cryptography
    + Confirmed Lecturers: Claude Crépeau, Alex Bredariol Grilo

- Course 3 (8h): Mathematical Aspects of Post-Quantum Techniques
    + Confirmed Lecturer: Thomas Debris-Alazard

I will just share some my interesting points that I learned from these courses, and I will not go into too much detail about the content of the courses, because it is too long and I think it is better to read the slides by yourself.

### FULLY HOMOMORPHIC ENCRYPTION - ILARIA CHILLOTTI

The first one is about FHE, I impressed by the leturer Ilaria Chilotti that She is very friendly and enthusiastic, and she explained the content very clearly. I learned a lot of things about FHE, especially the history of FHE, and the current state of FHE.

In this section, I studied from basic to advanced about FHE. 

Highly recommended to read the slides of this course, because it is very detailed and easy to understand.


### Functional Encryption for Inner Products - Ky Nguyen

The anothers that I wanna tell you about is Functional Encryption for Inner Products from Ky Nguyen. It made me impressed by the story behind the leturer. 

Ky Nguyen, he is a Vietnamese, a older brother in this field, used to be a student of the previous Viasm, and now he is a lecturer in this event. He shared his story about how he started to learn about crypto, and how he became a lecturer in this event. It was very inspiring for me, because I also want to become a lecturer in this event in the next few years.

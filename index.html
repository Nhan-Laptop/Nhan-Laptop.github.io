Behind the sky

The sky behind this page is procedural and drawn live with Three.js. One cloud of stars travels from a spiral galaxy to a ringed giant, a solar system, a lava world and an ocean planet. It is artistic, not an astronomical simulation.

Move through it and the stars you disturb turn into hex digits, then fade. With a mouse, press and hold to bend it. The button in the corner pauses it, and visitors who prefer reduced motion get a still sky. The Universe page has a calmer sky of its own.
